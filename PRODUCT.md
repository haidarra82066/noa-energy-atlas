# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static site, deployed as a public installable PWA on GitHub Pages.

## Users

Primary users are senior energy engineers, energy economists, legal and regulatory specialists, investors, and institutional decision-makers working with Lebanon's energy sector. They need to understand an instrument quickly, trace how it connects to other rules and institutions, and reach the authoritative source record without losing context.

## Product Purpose

Noa Energy Atlas makes Lebanon's energy-law and market framework navigable. It combines a source-backed knowledge graph, detailed instrument dossiers, and a dated energy briefing so users can move from a current development to the governing legal and institutional context.

Success means a user can identify the relevant instrument, understand its legal status and engineering or economic significance, inspect documented relationships, and open the original source within one continuous workflow.

## Positioning

The product joins verified legal records, professional interpretation, live relationship mapping, and bilingual market updates in one public atlas. Its differentiator is the navigable relationship layer between laws, policies, institutions, projects, and current developments, not a collection of isolated summaries.

## Operating Context

Users consult the atlas during project appraisal, policy analysis, regulatory due diligence, investment review, market monitoring, and preparation for technical or institutional discussions. The knowledge graph is the primary orientation surface; legal dossiers and the energy briefing provide depth and recency.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public access with no team login.
- English and Arabic interfaces in one validated content system, including RTL layout and original official names where available.
- Interactive graph with filtering, search, keyboard access, zoom, pan, pinch, draggable nodes, relationship inspection, and an accessible ledger alternative.
- Detailed law and instrument pages with legal, engineering, economic, implementation, evidence, and source sections.
- Dated energy updates linked back to affected instruments and original sources.
- Light and dark modes, offline support, and installable mobile-web behavior.
- Content must remain source-led, explicit about verification dates, and must not imply legal advice.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Noa Energy Atlas. Noa is a masculine phoenix companion connected to Lebanon, resilience, and the continuing review of the atlas. The established palette uses NuWatt-inspired deep navy, cyan-blue, and controlled coral-orange. The product should feel modern, authoritative, technically literate, and distinctly authored rather than machine-generated.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains validated instrument, relationship, source, claim, metric, and update ledgers; bilingual localization; detailed legal interpretations; verification agents; a reference implementation of energiewirtschaft-updates; and existing Noa mascot assets. No testimonials, customer claims, or commercial performance claims should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Relationships before isolated records.
2. Source and verification state remain visible.
3. Professional depth without sacrificing orientation.
4. Arabic is a first-class interface, not a translated afterthought.
5. Noa adds continuity and warmth without becoming a chatbot or weakening authority.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG-aligned contrast, visible focus, semantic controls, keyboard navigation, 44px minimum touch targets, reduced-motion behavior, mobile pinch zoom, and an accessible non-graph alternative. English and Arabic layouts must receive equal responsive and interaction QA.
