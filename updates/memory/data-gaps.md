# Persistent data gaps

- Comparable, current EDL supply hours by area and reporting period.
- Installed versus available generation capacity and plant-level constraints.
- Consistent public records for fuel deliveries, inventories and generation costs.
- Private-generator tariffs, consumption and substitution by distributed energy.
- A current national registry for installed distributed PV and battery storage.
- Import volumes, prices and specifications for PV modules, inverters and batteries.
- Project-level stage, finance, procurement and commissioning evidence for licensed utility solar.
- Hydropower availability, rehabilitation status and seasonal production.
- Distribution and transmission outage, loss and congestion data.
