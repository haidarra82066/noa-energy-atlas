# Source preferences

Use primary Lebanese legal and institutional evidence first: Official Gazette or official law text, Cabinet decisions, Parliament, Ministry of Energy and Water, Electricity Regulatory Authority, EDL, LCEC and the Lebanese Petroleum Administration. Use World Bank, UNDP, UNFCCC, IEA, IRENA and other multilaterals for project, finance and analytical evidence. Use research institutes and media for discovery or context, with explicit attribution and corroboration for consequential claims.

Record the exact URL, publisher, publication or event date where available, retrieval result, and research cutoff. A search result, social post, headline or memory is not sufficient evidence.
