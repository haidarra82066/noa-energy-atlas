# Assumption register

- Lebanon does not have a transparent liquid wholesale electricity market; do not fabricate German or EU-style spot-price signals.
- Installed capacity is not available capacity, and available capacity is not generation.
- A law in force may still require secondary rules and institutional capacity before its mechanisms become operational.
- An announcement, approval, licence, tender, award, financing, construction and commissioning are distinct project stages.
- A target or modeled pathway is not an observed outcome or forecast guarantee.
- Offshore prospective resources are not discovered resources, and discovered resources are not proved reserves.
- Currency, nominal/real basis, taxes, subsidies, measurement period and geographic coverage must accompany quantitative comparisons.
