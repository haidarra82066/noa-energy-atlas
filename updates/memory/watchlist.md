# Continuing watchlist

- Electricity Regulatory Authority: secondary regulations, tariff methodology, licences, market rules and grid codes.
- Law No. 318/2023: net-metering, peer-to-peer and direct-PPA procedures; EDL and ERA implementation responsibilities.
- EDL operations: available generation, fuel procurement, service hours, cost recovery, losses and network constraints.
- Lebanon Renewable Energy and System Reinforcement Project: effectiveness, procurement, disbursement, construction and commissioning milestones.
- NREAP 2025–2030: evidence that modeled capacity pathways are becoming financed or operational projects.
- Eleven utility-scale solar licences: distinguish approvals and licences from financing, construction and commissioning.
- Offshore petroleum: licensing, drilling, discoveries and resource estimates; never treat prospective resources as reserves.
