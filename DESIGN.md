# Noa Energy Atlas

## Design thesis

Noa Energy Atlas is an expert public reference product, not a marketing site and not a generic analytics dashboard. It should feel like an energy-system control room crossed with a legal atlas: authoritative enough for policy and investment work, spatial enough to make dependencies memorable, and calm enough for long-form evidence review.

Design dials: variance 7/10, motion 6/10, density 6/10.

## Visual grammar

- Deep navy (`#071c29` to `#0b2838`) anchors navigation, legal dossiers, and briefing records.
- NuWatt cyan (`#12a6b6`) marks active controls, verified relationships, and live graph signals.
- Warm coral (`#ef825f`) is reserved for priority actions, implementation friction, and Noa's energy signature.
- Cool paper (`#f2f8f8`) carries reading surfaces and keeps evidence legible.
- Borders and rules establish hierarchy. Shadows belong only to floating navigation, drawers, and the companion panel.
- Radii are 8-16px. Pill geometry is reserved for status, compact filters, and graph nodes.

## Typography

Use `Segoe UI Variable`, `Aptos`, and the system sans stack. Headlines are assertive but not theatrical. Labels use sentence case and restrained tracking. Body copy stays within a readable measure; numerals use tabular alignment where comparison matters.

## Layout and hierarchy

The desktop shell is an asymmetric command surface: a fixed dark rail, a compact view switcher, and a graph canvas that owns the remaining space. On mobile, navigation compresses into a deliberate header while graph count, controls, and fit-all framing stay visible. Laws and updates use ruled editorial records instead of repeated cards.

## Knowledge graph

The graph is the main navigation model. Nodes are clustered by legal family, connected by quiet neural paths, and animated by directional signal particles. Users can drag nodes; links follow in real time. Zoom, pan, pinch, filters, reset, keyboard selection, and the list alternative are first-class. Selecting any node opens a source-backed legal dossier without losing spatial context.

## Noa

Noa is a mature masculine Lebanese phoenix and a review companion, not a chat persona. Calm monitoring is the default state; attentive review appears when the panel opens. His color, proportions, crest, chest, and engraved feather language stay consistent across browser icon, app icon, navigation lockup, launcher, panel, and briefing.

## Motion language

Press feedback uses 140ms, local state 180ms, spatial state 240ms, and drawers up to 280ms. Camera moves ease out and remain interruptible. Hover motion applies only to fine pointers. Continuous graph signals and Noa's subtle idle breath are disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No scroll reveals or queued animation sequences.

## Bilingual behavior

English and Arabic use the same verified evidence ledger. Switching language updates document language, direction, alignment, navigation order, and curated instrument labels together. Original official titles remain visible where authoritative Arabic records exist. No legal translation is presented as a substitute for the source text.

## Accessibility floor

All controls remain keyboard operable with visible focus; status is never color-only; the graph has a complete list alternative; touch targets are at least 40px and generally 44px; content does not hide behind fixed navigation; page pinch zoom remains enabled; the application has no horizontal overflow at supported phone widths.
