# Noa Energy Atlas design system

The canonical system is documented in `DESIGN.md` at the project root. This interface is an energy control room crossed with a legal atlas: source-led, editorial, spatial, and built for senior energy engineers, economists, legal practitioners, investors, and public decision-makers.

Its product dials are variance 7/10, motion 6/10, and density 6/10. The visual system uses deep navy for legal authority, NuWatt cyan for live relationships and actions, coral for selective emphasis, and cool paper surfaces for evidence. Navigation behaves like an instrument panel; records use editorial rules instead of card stacks; the knowledge graph is the product's main spatial interface.

Noa is a named review companion, not a chatbot. The phoenix has a mature masculine expression and two purposeful states: calm monitoring and attentive review. English and Arabic share one evidence ledger, with native RTL behavior and original legal names retained where authoritative records exist.

Motion is functional and interruptible. Graph signals communicate relationships, camera movement maintains spatial context, drawers preserve causality, and active controls respond immediately. Motion reduces to near-static feedback under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
